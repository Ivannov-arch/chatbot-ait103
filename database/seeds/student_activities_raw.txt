Extra-Curricular Activities Department
Text
At Xiamen University Malaysia, learning extends far beyond the classroom. The Extra-Curricular Activities (ECA) Department plays a pivotal role in creating a dynamic campus experience by encouraging students to engage in meaningful activities that develop leadership, creativity, and personal growth. With more than 60 active student clubs and societies under five major categories, ECA offers countless opportunities to explore interests, make lifelong friendships, and shape your university journey.

Mission
Our mission is to empower every student at Xiamen University Malaysia to actively participate in extra-curricular activities throughout the year. We are committed to providing the guidance, resources, and opportunities needed to develop essential life skills such as leadership, teamwork, responsibility, and self-discipline, helping students thrive both on campus and beyond.
Text
What We Offer
Whether you're a passionate athlete, a performing arts enthusiast, a cultural explorer, or a future leader, the ECA Department is here to support your journey. We organize and oversee a wide range of student-led clubs, each offering unique experiences and events.
Text
How to Get Involved
Getting involved is simple! Students can join clubs during our
Recruitment Drive
, held twice a year, or reach out directly to club leaders through their club social media. Whether you're looking to start your own club or join an existing one, we’re here to help every step of the way.
